## File naming convention

`{family}-{weight}-{style}.ttf`

Where `weight` is `400` (regular) or `700` (bold), and `style` is
`normal` or `italic`. The four combinations cover the four base
styles each family ships with:

- 400-normal: Regular
- 400-italic: Italic
- 700-normal: Bold
- 700-italic: Bold Italic
